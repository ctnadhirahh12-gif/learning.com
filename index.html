THE GLOBAL GOALS

About SDG 4

Quality Education aims to make learning fair, inclusive and accessible, while giving everyone opportunities to keep learning throughout life.

04

Quality Education

Education helps people gain knowledge, unlock opportunities and contribute to a healthier and more progressive society.

Explore the SDG 4 targets

4.1

School education for all

Make free and quality primary and secondary education available to every girl and boy.

4.2

Early childhood learning

Give young children access to good care and pre-primary learning so they are ready for school.

4.3

Further education access

Ensure women and men have fair access to affordable technical, vocational and higher education.

4.4

Skills for work and life

Help young people and adults develop useful skills for jobs, careers and entrepreneurship.

4.5

Equal learning opportunities

Remove barriers and discrimination so vulnerable groups can access every level of education.

4.6

Reading and numeracy

Support young people and adults in gaining essential literacy and mathematics skills.

4.7

Learning for a better world

Teach skills and values linked to sustainability, human rights, equality, peace and cultural diversity.

4.a

Safe schools

Improve schools so they are safe, inclusive and suitable for learners with different needs.

4.b

Education scholarships

Expand scholarship opportunities for students from developing countries to continue higher education.

4.c

Qualified teachers

Increase the number of trained and qualified teachers, including through international support.
